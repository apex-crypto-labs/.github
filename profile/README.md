# Apex Research Labs

Inventors of **APEX-256**, the high-throughput, bare-metal stream cipher engineered for enterprise IoT and Cloud infrastructure.

### Access & Evaluation
APEX-256 and the APEX-AEAD architecture are proprietary, patent-pending commercial intellectual property. The core cryptographic engine is not distributed publicly.

To request evaluation access, undergo commercial due diligence, and execute the Apex Research Labs Evaluation NDA, please contact:
**👉 info@apex256.com**

Upon approval, your organization will be provisioned access to the official [APEX Developer Portal (apex256.io)](https://apex256.io) to download the SDKs and hardware toolchains.
